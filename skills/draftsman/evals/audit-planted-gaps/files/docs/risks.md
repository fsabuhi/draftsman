# Risks and guardrails

What could sink the product, and what keeps it from happening.

| Risk | Guardrail | Where it's handled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A tutor harms a student | The team vets every tutor before they can be booked, and sessions happen only in the app's video room | [Tutor profiles](features/tutor-profiles.md), [never.md](never.md) |
| Families don't come back after the first session | The after-launch gate measures second bookings | [Phases and gates](product.md#phases-and-gates) |
